METHOD & LIMITATIONS

Interpret the data responsibly

The source contains 5,000 records and 16 original columns. The Tableau-ready export contains 27 fields after adding derived grouping/display fields. The supplied audit reports zero missing cells and zero exact duplicate rows.

The health_risk_score field is constant at 100.0 and is not used as a differentiating KPI. Some measurement units and category-code meanings are undocumented. Associations do not establish causation.

Health360 is educational descriptive analytics, not a clinically validated diagnostic, prognostic or treatment system. Do not use it for individual medical decisions.
